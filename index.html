Biography

About
Miguel Colom

Born in Madrid and trained in Madrid and Berlin, Miguel Colom moves naturally between the roles of soloist, chamber musician and concertmaster. He has appeared as a soloist with orchestras such as the Konzerthausorchester Berlin and the Spanish National Orchestra, and performed at Carnegie Hall in New York as winner of the Orpheus Award.

He is concertmaster of the Spanish National Orchestra (OCNE), a member of the Trío VibrArt and, since 2021, professor at the Escuela Superior de Música Reina Sofía in Madrid.
