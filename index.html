About
Miguel Colom

Born in Madrid, Miguel Colom is one of the leading Spanish violinists of his generation. Trained in Madrid and Berlin, he moves naturally between the roles of soloist, chamber musician and concertmaster, and has appeared at venues such as the Berlin Philharmonie, the Konzerthaus Berlin, the Auditorio Nacional in Madrid and Carnegie Hall in New York.

He is First Concertmaster of the Spanish National Orchestra, a member of the VibrArt Trio, and passes on his experience as Associate Professor of the Telefónica Violin Chair at the Reina Sofía School of Music.
