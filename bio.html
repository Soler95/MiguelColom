About
Miguel Colom

Miguel Colom is currently concertmaster of the Spanish National Orchestra (OCNE). He is a prize winner of the Max Rostal International Competition and has won First Prize at the Ibolyka Gyarfas Violin Competition Berlin, First Prize at the Competition of Juventudes Musicales de España, the Conservatorio Statale di Fermo award at the Andrea Postacchini International Competition in Italy, and the First Prize, Special Award and Prize for the best performer of Spanish music at the “Villa de Llanes” International Violin Competition. He has performed at Carnegie Hall in New York as the winner of the Orpheus Award at the Manhattan International Competition 2021.

As a soloist, Colom has played with the Konzerthausorchester Berlin, Orquesta y Coro Nacionales de España, Orquesta Sinfónica de RTVE, Orquesta Sinfónica de Barcelona y Nacional de Catalunya, Kölner Kammerorchester, ADDA Simfònica, Real Orquesta Sinfónica de Sevilla, The World Orchestra, Orquesta Sinfónica Freixenet, Orquesta Sinfónica de El Salvador, Orquesta Sinfónica del Vallès and the Humboldt Universität Orchestra, amongst others.

In parallel to his career as a soloist, Miguel Colom is an active chamber musician, having performed with artists such as Antje Weithaas, Rainer Schmidt, Denis Pascal, Cuarteto Quiroga, Cuarteto Casals, Artemis Quartet, Duo del Valle, Karl Leister and Pepe Romero, amongst others. He is currently a member of the Trío VibrArt, with pianist Juan Pérez Floristán and cellist Fernando Arias. He is regularly invited as concertmaster by renowned orchestras such as the Konzerthausorchester Berlin, NDR Radiophilharmonie, Wiener Symphoniker, Orquesta Sinfónica de Madrid, OBC, Orquesta del Palau de les Arts Valencia and Orquesta Sinfónica de Tenerife.

Born in Madrid, he began his violin studies with Sergio Castro and Anna Baget, then continued at the Escuela Superior de Música Reina Sofía under Rainer Schmidt, at the Hochschule für Musik “Hanns Eisler” Berlin with Antje Weithaas and at the Berlin University of the Arts (UdK) with Nora Chastain. He has also taken part in master classes with Eberhard Feltz, Ferenc Rados, Ana Chumachenco, Claudio Martínez Mehner and Mauricio Fuks.

Since 2021 he has been a professor at the Escuela Superior de Música Reina Sofía in Madrid, and he also teaches at the Barenboim-Said Orchestral Studies School in Seville.
